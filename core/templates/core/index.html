Our Core Services

Web Development

We build fast, secure websites and web applications tailored to how your business actually works. From company sites to customer portals, every build is responsive, tested and easy to maintain.

Mobile App Development

We design and build Android and iOS apps that your customers will want to keep on their phones. Offline support, mobile payments and push notifications come built in where you need them.

IT Consulting

We help you choose the right technology before you spend money on it. Our consultants review your current setup and give you a clear, prioritised roadmap.

System Integration

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions we hear most from new clients

What services does Elevora offer?

We offer web development, mobile app development, UI/UX design, IT consulting, system integration, and cloud and cybersecurity services. You can work with us on a single service or combine several into one project.

How long does it take to build a website or app?

It depends on the size and features of the project. A simple business website can take a few weeks, while a custom web application or mobile app takes longer. After we understand your goals, we give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

How much does a project cost?

Every project is different, so we quote based on your needs instead of using fixed packages. Tell us what you want to build, and we will send a clear estimate with no hidden costs.

Do you provide support after the project is launched?

Yes. Launch is only the beginning. We can monitor, maintain, update, and secure your product after go-live, so it keeps running smoothly as your business grows.

How do I get started with Elevora?

Reach out through our contact section with a short description of your idea. We will arrange a conversation to understand your goals, then share a plan and estimate for your approval.
