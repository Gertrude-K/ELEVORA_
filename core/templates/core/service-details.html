Our Core Services

Elevora helps businesses plan, build and run the technology they depend on. Whether you need a new website, a mobile app, systems that finally talk to each other or a secure move to the cloud, our team covers every stage from strategy and design to development and support.

Web Development

Your website is often the first place customers meet your business, so it has to load quickly, work on every device and make it easy to take the next step. We build everything from company websites to full web applications such as booking systems, customer portals and online stores. Each project uses proven frameworks, clean code and automated testing, so it stays reliable and easy to extend as your needs change.

What's included
  • Responsive websites and progressive web apps
  • Custom web applications, dashboards and portals
  • E-commerce with secure online and mobile-money payments
  • Search-friendly structure and fast page loads

Mobile App Development

Many of your customers do everything on their phones, often on slow or patchy connections. We build cross-platform Android and iOS apps that work well in those real conditions, with offline modes, low data use and simple, familiar navigation. We handle the full journey, from design and development to testing on real devices and publishing to Google Play and the App Store.

What's included
  • Cross-platform apps with Flutter or React Native
  • Offline mode and low-data optimisation
  • Mobile-money payments, SMS and push notifications
  • App store publishing and post-launch updates

IT Consulting

Technology decisions are expensive to get wrong. Our consultants start by understanding your goals, your team and the systems you already have. We then review your infrastructure, software and processes, and give you a clear, prioritised roadmap that shows what to fix first, what to build and what to buy, so every shilling you spend on technology moves the business forward.

What's included
  • IT audits and technology health checks
  • Digital transformation roadmaps
  • Software and vendor selection
  • Process automation opportunities

System Integration

Most businesses run on several systems that do not talk to each other, so staff end up copying data between spreadsheets, accounting software and customer records. We connect those systems through secure APIs and automated data pipelines, so information entered once appears everywhere it is needed. That means fewer errors, faster reporting and a single, reliable view of your business.

What's included
  • API design and third-party integrations
  • ERP, CRM, POS and accounting connections
  • Payment gateway and mobile-money integration
  • Data migration from legacy systems

UI/UX Design

A product only succeeds if people can use it without thinking twice. Our designers start with research, talking to your users and studying how they work, then turn what they learn into wireframes, visual designs and clickable prototypes. You can test and refine the experience with real users before development starts, which saves time, cost and rework later.

What's included
  • User research and journey mapping
  • Wireframes and interactive prototypes
  • Visual design and design systems
  • Usability testing and accessibility reviews

Cloud & Cybersecurity

Outages and data breaches cost businesses money and customer trust. We migrate your systems to reliable cloud platforms, set up automatic backups and monitoring, and lock down access with strong authentication and encryption. Regular security reviews and clear policies help keep your business, and your customers' data, protected as you grow.

What's included
  • Cloud migration and hosting on AWS, Azure or Google Cloud
  • Automated backups and disaster recovery
  • Security audits and vulnerability testing
  • Multi-factor authentication and access control
